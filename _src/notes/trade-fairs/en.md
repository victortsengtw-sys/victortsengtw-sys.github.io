When you first approach the market in Côte d'Ivoire (in West Africa, French-speaking, the world's largest cocoa producer), attending an industry fair is often a better first step than setting up locally straight away: in just a few days you can meet government departments, buyers, distributors and peers. The events below are limited to those that can be verified through public official or media sources; always check the organisers' latest announcements for dates.

## At a glance

| Event | Sector | Latest edition | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| SARA International Agriculture and Animal Resources Show | Agriculture, livestock, agro-processing | 7th edition, 23 May to 1 June 2025 | Farm machinery, agricultural inputs, food-processing businesses |
| SITLA Abidjan International Tourism and Leisure Fair | Tourism, leisure, food and beverage | 10th edition, 19 to 21 September 2025 | Hospitality, travel and travel-tech businesses |
| Jnec-CI National Cashew Exporters' Days | Cashew industry | 6th edition, 20 to 21 January 2026 | Nut traders, processing-equipment suppliers |
| IvoirTech Forum | Start-ups, digital transformation | July 2025 (officially announced date) | Software, ICT and digital-service businesses |
| Africa CEO Forum | Pan-African business leaders' forum | 3 to 4 May 2027 in Abidjan | Senior executives, investors, financial institutions |

## Highlights of each event

**SARA** (Salon International de l'Agriculture et des Ressources Animales) is held at the Abidjan Exhibition Centre (Parc des Expositions d'Abidjan). The 2025 edition drew 915 exhibitors from 32 countries, with China as guest of honour and a focus on agro-processing and food sovereignty. The organisers' provisional figures reported about 410,000 visits and about XOF 406 billion in investment intentions and contracts. For makers of farm machinery, fertilisers, irrigation or food-processing equipment, it is the most direct showcase available.

**SITLA** (Salon International du Tourisme et des Loisirs d'Abidjan) returned in 2025 after a five-year break, also at the Abidjan Exhibition Centre. All 31 of the country's regions took part, themes included tourism digitalisation and local gastronomy, and entry was free.

**Jnec-CI** (Journées nationales des exportateurs de cajou de Côte d'Ivoire): the 6th edition in 2026 was opened by the director general of the cashew regulator, the CCA, with the cashew exporters' association (Aec-CI) also involved. More than 3,000 people attended, and the Vietnam Cashew Association was guest of honour. It is a window onto the cashew supply chain and processing investment.

**IvoirTech Forum** is organised by the Ministry of Digital Transformation. It was announced at GITEX Africa in Morocco in April 2025, with the aim of bringing together start-ups and digital-transformation players.

**Africa CEO Forum** is co-organised by the Jeune Afrique media group and the International Finance Corporation (IFC), part of the World Bank Group. Its 12th edition was held in Abidjan in May 2025; it moved to Kigali in 2026 and will return to Abidjan in May 2027, when the organisers expect more than 2,800 participants. Côte d'Ivoire's investment promotion centre (CEPICI) has also held an "Invest in Côte d'Ivoire" side event during the forum.

## Preparing to exhibit

French-language brochures and business cards are basic essentials for communicating at local fairs. It is also worth contacting CEPICI or the relevant sector authority in advance to find out whether matchmaking sessions will run during the event. Follow-up visits after the fair are the key step in turning the business cards you collected into actual partnerships.

## One line for Asian companies

Pick the fair most relevant to your industry and visit it first as an attendee — that tells you more about whether the market suits you than renting a stand straight away. Once your direction is clear, plan your exhibition and follow-up visits.
